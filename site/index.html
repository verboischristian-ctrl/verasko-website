It checks your official documents first

Most AI assistants treat every page the same. Verasko ranks your sources, so an old FAQ page never overrules your terms.

  1. Your official documents

    Prices, dates, deadlines and conditions come only from here, and must match the text.

    Terms of sale, price lists, policies, product sheets.

  2. Your website and FAQ

    Used for simple questions, like how to do something or where to find it. Never against your official documents.

  3. No reliable source

    Verasko does not guess. Your form goes through as usual, and the question goes into your weekly report.

Three possible outcomes for each message

Your contact form keeps working exactly as before. Verasko only adds an answer when it can back it up.

Clear answer
Shown to the visitor before the form is sent, with its source. They can still send their message.
Partial answer
The message reaches your team as usual, with a suggested reply attached.
No answer
The message reaches your team as usual. The missing information is noted for you.

Every week, a short report

No dashboard to log into. Each week you receive an email with:

  • the questions Verasko answered, and how visitors reacted;
  • the questions your documents do not answer yet;
  • pages on your website that contradict your official documents;
  • new answers added to the help block on your website.

Questions before you start

Something else on your mind? Write to hello@verasko.com.

Does Verasko replace my contact form?

No. Your form stays where it is and keeps working. Verasko only adds an answer before the message is sent, when it can back it up. Visitors can always send their message.

What happens when Verasko is not sure?

It does not answer. The message reaches you as usual, and the missing information appears in your weekly report, so you can add it to your documents.

How does Verasko avoid wrong answers?

It answers only from the documents you mark as official. Each fact is checked a second time by a separate AI model, and prices, dates, deadlines and conditions must match your documents. If a check fails, the visitor sees no answer.

Will it work with my website?

Verasko is added with one line of code in the footer of your website. During the pilot program, we check with you that it works with your contact form before it goes live.

What happens to my visitors' messages?

The messages typed in your form are personal data. Verasko processes them on your behalf, only to answer and to prepare your report, and stores them in the European Union.
